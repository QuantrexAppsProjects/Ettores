Ettore's European Bakery & Restaurant

Since 1985 on Fair Oaks

In 1985 a Swiss-trained pastry chef, Ettore Ravazzolo, put his name on a small croissant shop in Sacramento and began baking the cakes he grew up with.

He arrived from Switzerland with $350. The croissant recipe from those first mornings is still the one we bake, and the cinnamon rolls have been on the counter for more than thirty years.

Today the same kitchen fills the pastry case before the doors open, runs a café from breakfast to dinner with a wood-burning brick oven, and builds wedding cakes for families who have been coming here for generations.

Who bakes here now

Since1985

OwnerLisa Calk, since 2018

WhereArden Arcade, Sacramento

Lisa Calk worked at Ettore's for fourteen years before she bought it in 2018. The recipes stayed, and so did the families who come back for a birthday cake every year.

The same kitchen now bakes for the café, the pastry case, the specialty cake department and catering orders across Sacramento.
